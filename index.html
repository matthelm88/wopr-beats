    > HOW TO PLAY

    QUICK START

    Pick a style, set the DEFCON level and tap SHALL WE PLAY A GAME? Tap RUN to hear it. DEFCON 5 gives classic grooves; DEFCON 1 goes wild.

    SHAPE IT

    Tap any step to turn it on or off. Tap a sound name to select it, then LOCK it to keep it when you generate again, or SOLO it to hear it alone. VARY changes a few steps on the current pattern. The arrows at the top step back and forward through your recent generations.

    PLAY ALONG

    Select a sound and tap SOLO. Let it loop until you can feel it, then record it live on the PO. If it does not land, erase and try again.

    COPY TO THE PO

    The PO VIEW tab shows a 4x4 grid laid out like the PO buttons. MONO squeezes the pattern to one sound per step: kick wins, then snare, metal, open hat and closed hat. Tap a sound to see only its steps. Set HEAR to PO MONO to hear what will fit.

    LONG PATTERNS AND FILLS

    32, 48 and 64 steps become 2, 3 or 4 PO patterns. BUILD ORDER tells you which one to program first and what to change after copying it. Put the FILL in its own pattern and chain it after the groove. CHAIN plays the groove three times, then the fill.

    NO SOUND?

    Flip the iPhone silent switch off, then tap RUN again.

    MIDI

    MIDI FILE saves the main pattern as a .mid file for other apps and machines. Kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42, open hat 46, metal 56, on channel 10. The download works from your own web address; Claude's preview blocks downloads.
