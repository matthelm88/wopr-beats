    > HOW TO PLAY

    QUICK START

    Pick a style, set DEFCON and tap NEW BEAT. Tap RUN at the bottom to hear it. DEFCON 5 gives classic grooves; DEFCON 1 goes wild. Every button with a die is a roll: tap it again for a new result.

    STYLES

    BIG BEAT: Chemical Brothers, Prodigy. IDM: Autechre and glitch. INDUSTRIAL: Skinny Puppy, Front Line Assembly, KMFDM, Ministry. JUNKYARD: Einstürzende Neubauten metal percussion.

    SHAPE IT

    Tap any step to turn it on or off. Tap a sound name to select it, then LOCK it to keep it through new rolls, or SOLO it to hear it alone. NEW BEAT throws everything out and rolls a whole new beat across every pattern. TWEAK keeps your beat and changes only a few steps on the pattern you are looking at. The arrows at the top step back and forward through your recent results.

    FILLS

    Pick a pattern tab, then tap FILL. The second half of that pattern turns into a fill. Keep tapping FILL to roll new fills on that pattern only. UNFILL puts the original groove back. On a 16-step loop, FILL adds Pattern 2 for the fill.

    PLAY ALONG

    On PO VIEW, tap a sound, then SOLO IN MONO. It loops only the steps that fit on the PO for that sound. Learn it by ear, record it live, then tap the next sound and the solo follows.

    COPY TO THE PO

    PO VIEW shows a 4x4 grid laid out like the PO buttons. MONO squeezes the pattern to one sound per step: kick wins, then snare, metal, open hat and closed hat. Set HEAR to MONO to hear what will fit. BUILD ORDER tells you which pattern to program first and what to change after copying it.

    FOLLOW

    With FOLLOW on, the pattern tabs jump along with the playhead. Tapping a tab yourself while it plays turns FOLLOW off.

    NO SOUND?

    Flip the iPhone silent switch off, then tap RUN again.

    MIDI

    MIDI FILE saves the pattern as a .mid file. Kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42, open hat 46, metal 56, on channel 10. The download works from your own web address; Claude's preview blocks downloads.
