    > HOW TO PLAY

    QUICK START

    Tap STYLE to change style, set DEFCON and tap NEW BEAT. Tap play at the bottom to hear it from the start of Pattern 1; tap stop to end it. DEFCON 5 gives classic grooves; DEFCON 1 goes wild. Every button with a die is a roll: tap it again for a new result.

    STYLES AND KITS

    BIG BEAT: Chemical Brothers, Prodigy. IDM: Autechre and glitch. INDUSTRIAL: Skinny Puppy, Front Line Assembly, KMFDM, Ministry. JUNKYARD: Einstürzende Neubauten metal percussion. PUNK: fast 8th-note drive and skank beats. FUNK: syncopated kicks, ghost snares and 16th hats with swing. Each style has three synthesized kits; tap KIT to hear the next one. BIG BEAT: 808 BOOM, BREAKS, 909 ACID. IDM: GLITCH, MICRO, CRUSHED. INDUSTRIAL: FACTORY, EBM, PISTON. JUNKYARD: SCRAP, PIPES, OIL DRUM. PUNK: GARAGE, D-BEAT, HARDCORE. FUNK: BREAKBEAT, DISCO, ELECTRO. The METAL row is each kit's fifth sound: cowbell, tambourine, clap, blip, anvil, pipe, crash or ride.

    SHAPE IT

    The small grid shows all five sounds. Tap a row to pick a sound, then tap the big 4x4 pads (laid out like the PO buttons) to turn its steps on or off. With a sound picked, then LOCK it to keep it through new rolls, or SOLO it to hear it alone. NEW BEAT throws everything out (up to 128 steps, tap STEPS to change) and rolls a whole new beat across every pattern. TWEAK keeps your beat and changes only a few steps on the pattern you are looking at. The arrows at the top step back and forward through your recent results.

    MOVES

    Tap MOVES to swap the pads for the moves panel; tap PADS to go back. Section moves rewrite the pattern you are on and always start from its original groove, so tapping one again re-rolls it. FILL: snare-led fill. BUILD: a roll that gets denser. BREAK: breakdown with no kick. GAP: silence before a drop. DROP: the groove with a marker on step 1. HALF: half-time feel. STRIP: one or two sounds, for intros and outros. STOP: stabs, then silence. ANSWER: keeps the last pattern's kick and moves the snare. On a 16-step loop, FILL and ANSWER add Pattern 2.

    SPICES

    Spices are small tweaks that stack: PUSH moves a kick or snare a step early, O-HAT adds open hats, RATCHET repeats a hit, M-GUN fires kicks to the end of the bar, GHOST adds extra snares, EUCLID spreads hits evenly, POLY lays a 3, 5, 6 or 7 step loop across the whole beat. RATCHET, EUCLID and POLY use the SOUND button. RESET puts the pattern back to its original groove.

    SONG

    ROLL A SONG picks a song shape such as STRIP, GROOVE, BUILD, DROP and rolls every pattern to match. Tabs show each pattern's role. Repeated sections are exact copies, so on the PO you program fewer slots and repeat them in the chain. BUILD ORDER on PO VIEW lists the slots, what to copy from, and the chain.

    PERFORM

    Punch-in effects like a Pocket Operator. Tap a pad and the effect starts right away. Tap it again, or tap a different pad, and the change waits for the end of the 16-step pattern: the current effect keeps going (blinking), the next one shows dashed, then they swap on the downbeat. ALL OFF also waits: the effect finishes the pattern, then the plain beat comes back on the downbeat. Only the stop button cuts everything at once. The beat keeps running underneath, so you always land back in time. HOLD MODE works like a PO: hold a pad, and letting go ends the effect at the end of the pattern. Hold a second pad to queue a switch; lift it before the downbeat to cancel. Pressing a pad while stopped starts the beat.

    EFFECT BANKS

    48 effects from Teenage Engineering gear, 16 per bank. The small tag on each pad says which device it comes from. 1 · K.O: the PO-33 effects in PO-33 order (LOOP 16 to REVERSE), plus the PO-35 STUTTER SWEEP. 2 · TONE: PO-35 TRANCE GATE, HALF RATE and 16-BAR BUILD UP; PO-32 PITCH BEND UP, PITCH LFO, SQUASH, BOUNCING BALL and EQ SWEEP; PO-12 LOWPASS and HIPASS filters and sweeps, DISTORTION, BIT CRUSH, DELAY and FEEDBACK. 3 · CHOP: PO-12 NOTE SHUFFLE; PO-28 FILL-IN, GLITCH, BLINDS and NOISE; PO-24 DISTORT LOW and HIGH; OP-Z DUCK and STEREO; EP-133 TAPE STOP, TREMOLO, PITCH RANDOM, FILTER LFO and SLICE. These are synthesized versions built for this app, so they will not sound exactly like the hardware. A bank button lights up when its effect is playing, so you can change banks mid-effect.

    RECORD EFFECTS

    Tap REC (it turns red), then play pads. Every step an effect plays on gets written into the pattern, and from then on it plays back by itself every time the loop comes round. Recording adds on top: steps where you play nothing keep what was there, so you can build effects up over several passes. A pad you play live always wins over the recorded effect. Red marks on the strip under the pattern counter show recorded steps. CLEAR FX wipes every recorded effect. NEW BEAT also starts with none.

    TEMP SAVE AND RESET

    TEMP SAVE stores the beat as it is right now, including any recorded effects. RESET goes back to it: while playing it waits for the end of the current pattern (blinking), then reloads on the downbeat with no gap, and REC turns off. If you tap REC with no temp save yet, one is taken first, so RESET can always undo a take. The temp save stays on this phone between visits. For a performance: build the beat, TEMP SAVE, record effects, play the result, then RESET to drop back to the clean version on the beat. SAVE at the top keeps a copy for good, recorded effects included.

    PLAY ALONG

    On PO VIEW, tap a sound, then SOLO IN MONO. It loops only the steps that fit on the PO for that sound. Learn it by ear, record it live, then tap the next sound and the solo follows.

    COPY TO THE PO

    PO VIEW shows a 4x4 grid laid out like the PO buttons. MONO squeezes the pattern to one sound per step: kick wins, then snare, metal, open hat and closed hat. Set HEAR to MONO to hear what will fit. STEP LIST shows BUILD ORDER: which PO slots to program, which to copy (select the slot, hold WRITE + PATTERN, press the new slot) and the chain (hold PATTERN, press the slots in order).

    FOLLOW

    With FOLLOW on, the pattern tabs jump along with the playhead. Tapping a tab yourself while it plays turns FOLLOW off.

    SAVES ON YOUR PHONE

    Saves live in this browser. On the SAVED tab, EXPORT makes a file and opens the share sheet, so you can put it in Files, email it or AirDrop it. LOAD FILE picks that file and adds its saves back, skipping any already here.

    NO SOUND?

    Flip the iPhone silent switch off, then tap play again.

    MIDI

    MIDI FILE saves the pattern as a .mid file. Kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42, open hat 46, metal 56, on channel 10. The download works from your own web address; Claude's preview blocks downloads.
